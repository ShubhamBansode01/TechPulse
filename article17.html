Infinix Note 60 Pro Review: A Shift to Qualcomm Silicon

June 12, 2026 By Tech Team 6 Min Read

Infinix has shaken up its smartphone lineup with the introduction of the Infinix Note 60 Pro. Historically running exclusively on MediaTek Dimensity processors, this new entrant introduces Qualcomm silicon alongside a radical dual-display concept and specialized magnetic ecosystems. Below is a comprehensive technical evaluation of what makes the Note 60 Pro one of the most intriguing devices of the year.

1. Design and the Rear LED Matrix Display

The device breaks conventional design rules by offering a dual-display ecosystem. The rear panel is dominated by a busy aesthetic consisting of **288 customizable LEDs** forming an Active Matrix Display. This secondary panel displays notifications, ambient music rhythms, battery charging status, or animated digital pets. It also features a customizable RGB accent strip.

The phone weighs an impressive **201.7 grams**, which is lightweight considering it packs a massive 6500mAh battery cell. Constructed around a strong metal frame with a premium polycarbonate rear panel, the device holds an IP64 water-and-dust resistance certification. The box includes a high-quality MagSafe-compatible hard case that facilitates ecosystem accessory pairing.

2. Front Display Parameters

The primary display panel is outstanding, presenting minimal bezels and a tightly controlled bottom chin. It features a **6.78-inch 1.5K 10-bit AMOLED display** running at a hyper-smooth **144Hz refresh rate**. Outdoor readability is effortless thanks to a peak brightness of up to 4500 nits (and 1600 nits in High Brightness Mode). Furthermore, a flicker-free low-brightness implementation prevents late-night eye strain.

4. Hardware Performance & Gaming

The switch to Qualcomm architecture yields incredible stability, maintaining an almost flawless **100% GPU and CPU stability loop** under peak stressing thresholds. The device is Call of Duty certified, unlocking optimized **120 FPS performance in COD** and **90 FPS parameters within BGMI**. Internal temperatures stay within check thanks to an integrated vapor cooling chamber.

5. Software Lifecycle & Specialized AI Button

The Note 60 Pro operates on **XOS 16 based on Android 16**. Markedly improving upon prior upgrade policies, Infinix commits to **3 years of major Android updates and 5 years of security deployments**. The user interface offers deep optimization and clean navigation layout pipelines.

The hardware includes a customizable physical **AI button** on the side rail, which can toggle immediate system actions like muting notifications or firing screenshots. On the digital front, the built-in Folax Assistant opens access to Large Language Models like Grok and ChatGPT, alongside Circle to Search functionality and live voice/text transcribing pipelines.

6. The MagPower Audio Ecosystem

The phone offers excellent connectivity across Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.4, an IR Blaster, and a physical dual nano-SIM setup. It highlights **proximity-based Bluetooth calling**, enabling seamless peer-to-peer voice calling even without cellular network coverage.

The audio experience is further elevated via an auxiliary **MagPower portable companion speaker** bundled in introductory offers. The speaker connects magnetically over MagSafe to form an ergonomic desk viewing stand, doubling as an integrated 3-in-1 tool that outputs rich sound while charging the phone via standard USB Type-C passthrough.

7. Optic Performance Breakdown

The primary photographic configuration features a **50MP primary wide sensor** alongside an **8MP ultrawide lens**, while the selfie camera clocks in with a **13MP front sensor**. The software processes images to emphasize punchy color gamuts and handles native 2x digital crop zooming metrics comfortably. Crucially, the phone handles native **4K 30FPS video capture across both front and rear configurations** seamlessly.

Final Pricing Analysis

The Infinix Note 60 Pro targets an effective introductory retail price of **₹28,999** (with structured bank offers lowering standard entry barriers). Bundled consumer perks include the external soundbar setup, a 18-month Google Gemini Pro premium tier access subscription framework, and a dedicated 5TB allotment of cloud database allocation storage. Offering highly competitive performance baselines via its distinct Qualcomm core and innovative active secondary matrix display layout, it stands as a unique value proposition.
