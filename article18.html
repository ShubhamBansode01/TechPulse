Motorola Signature Review: The Ultimate Flagship Killer?

June 12, 2026 By Tech Team 6 Min Read

When the Motorola Signature series was first announced for the European market with an astronomical price tag, many assumed it would be out of reach. However, its official Indian launch has entirely changed the narrative. Sitting confidently above the standard Edge series, the Motorola Signature is a true flagship packing top-tier specifications at a surprisingly aggressive price point. Let's dive deep into why this might just be the best smartphone under ₹60,000.

1. Design, Build, and Durability

Motorola has mastered the art of creating sleek hardware. The Signature series is incredibly thin at just 6.99mm and weighs a mere 187 grams—an engineering marvel for a phone packing a massive 6.8-inch display and a 5200mAh battery.

The build quality leaves absolutely no room for complaints:

  • Materials: Features a solid metal frame combined with a twill-inspired finish (and a unique denim-style texture option).
  • Glass Protection: The front is shielded by Corning Gorilla Glass Victus 2.
  • Durability Certifications: It goes beyond the standard IP68/IP69 dust and water resistance, boasting a rigorous Military-Grade 810H certification. We simulated standard 5 to 6-foot drop tests, and the metal frame handled impacts flawlessly.

2. Display and Multimedia Excellence

The screen-to-body ratio is exceptional with near-invisible bezels and a slight curve on all four sides. The device sports a large 6.8-inch Extreme AMOLED LTPO display with a flexible refresh rate adjusting from 1Hz up to a blistering 165Hz (ideal for competitive mobile gaming).

Visibility is never an issue thanks to an eye-watering peak brightness of 6200 nits. When combined with the Bose-tuned Dolby Atmos dual stereo speakers, the multimedia experience easily rivals—and perhaps beats—devices that cost twice as much.

3. Flagship Hardware and Battery

Gaming performance is flawlessly stable. Titles like BGMI and COD Mobile run comfortably at 120FPS, while Genshin Impact holds a steady 58-59 FPS. What stands out is Motorola's new 6000mm² Copper Vapor Chamber combined with a liquid metal cooling system—heat dissipation is incredibly fast, keeping CPU/GPU throttling to a minimum.

4. Camera System: A Top 10 DXOMARK Contender

Motorola has historically struggled slightly with flagship cameras, but not anymore. This device proudly sits in the Top 10 of the DXOMARK rankings. The setup includes:

  • 50MP Primary Sensor: Uses the highly acclaimed Sony LYT-828 sensor (commonly found on phones costing over ₹80,000).
  • 50MP Telephoto: Offers a stunning 3X optical zoom (equivalent to an 85mm portrait focal length) producing impeccable bokeh and Pantone-validated skin tones.
  • 50MP Ultrawide: Captures a massive 122° Field of View.
  • 50MP Front Camera: Ensuring ultra-sharp selfies.

Videography is equally impressive, supporting 8K at 30FPS on the main sensor, and 4K at 60FPS across all other lenses (including the front). It even supports Dolby Vision recording, showcasing exceptional dynamic range.

5. Software and Connectivity Features

The device boots on Hello UI based on Android 16. It is completely bloatware-free, offering one of the cleanest Android experiences available today. Motorola promises a staggering 7 years of major OS updates and 7 years of security patches.

The Moto AI integration is genuinely useful, featuring tools like 'Catch Me Up', 'Remember This', and seamless integration with external LLMs like Gemini or Perplexity via a dedicated AI hardware key. Furthermore, the phone is a global powerhouse supporting an incredible 24 5G bands, Wi-Fi 7, NFC, and UWB (Ultra Wideband) tracking.

Final Verdict: Should You Buy It?

With an MRP of ₹65,000 and effective pricing falling under ₹60,000 with bank and exchange offers, the Motorola Signature is an absolute steal. From its jaw-dropping display and refined Sony LYT-828 cameras to its impeccable battery/charging combo and 7-year update guarantee, it leaves no stone unturned. If you are looking for a premium flagship without the ₹1 Lakh+ price tag, your search ends here.
