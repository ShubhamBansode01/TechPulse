Vivo V60 Review: Specifications, ZEISS Camera, and Performance

June 12, 2026 By Tech Team 5 Min Read

Six months after the release of the V50, the Vivo V60 has arrived. While maintaining the core DNA of its predecessors, the V60 offers measurable upgrades across almost every department. Built primarily for the offline market where design and camera prowess reign supreme, this device brings substantial AI features and ZEISS-tuned optics. Here is everything you need to know about the new Vivo V60.

1. Design and Build Quality

The Vivo V60 feels premium in the hand and features an opaque case with a modestly sized camera module. Weighing in at roughly 201g, it has some heft but remains comfortable to hold. Vivo has included "Diamond Shield" glass protection to prevent shattering from accidental drops.

The phone is available in three colorways:

  • Gold & Blue: Feature a premium glass back.
  • Grey: Features a durable polycarbonate back.

Additionally, it carries an impressive IP68 and IP69 certification, meaning it can theoretically survive underwater photography for short periods without hardware failure.

2. Display and Multimedia

The front showcases a 6.77-inch FHD+ display. Vivo opted for a "micro quad-curved" screen design, resulting in a phenomenal screen-to-body ratio. While it is not a 1.5K display, it does support HDR10+, making it ideal for streaming high-dynamic-range content on Netflix and YouTube. Combined with proper stereo speakers, the multimedia experience is well above average.

3. Performance and Hardware

Moving away from the 7th Gen 3 chip used in the last three generations, the Vivo V60 is powered by the new Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 processor. It comfortably hits an AnTuTu benchmark score of nearly 1 Million (approx. 985K), representing a 20-25% performance jump over its predecessor.

However, Vivo made a slight compromise in memory speeds by using LPDDR4X RAM and UFS 2.2 Storage. Despite this, thermal management is excellent. Heavy titles like Genshin Impact run smoothly at 45-46 FPS for 30-minute sessions without excessive throttling or overheating.

4. Massive Battery Life

One of the biggest highlights is the massive 6500mAh battery, paired with a 90W Flash charge adapter included in the box. In practical testing, the phone yields roughly 9.5 to 10 hours of screen-on time, easily lasting a normal user 1.5 to 2 days on a single charge.

5. Software and Connectivity

The V60 runs on Funtouch OS based on Android 15. Vivo promises an excellent update lifecycle: 4 major Android updates and 6 years of security patches. It includes a native dialer (allowing call recording without announcements) and robust AI features like Wi-Fi Smart Call Assistant for live translations, and highly effective spam call blocking.

Connectivity features include Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.4, 11 5G bands, NFC, and an IR blaster. Note that the device uses a dual nano-SIM tray and does not support eSIM.

6. ZEISS Cameras: The Main Event

The camera setup is a significant upgrade over the V50, prominently featuring ZEISS tuning. The rear houses a 50MP primary sensor, a 50MP ultrawide sensor, and a 3X telephoto lens. The selfie camera is also a massive 50MP shooter.

  • Portrait Photography: Offers focal lengths ranging from 24mm to 100mm, ensuring natural subject proportions.
  • Zoom Capabilities: Supports up to 100x digital zoom, though it performs optimally up to 10x (perfect for a "Stage Mode" at concerts).
  • Video: Shoots in 4K at 30FPS on all sensors, though 4K 60FPS is noticeably absent.
  • AI Integration: Features AI 4 Seasons Portrait, AI Reflection Remover, AI Magic Move, and AI Image Expander.

Final Verdict and Pricing

The Vivo V60 comes in four variants: 8GB/128GB, 8GB/256GB, 12GB/256GB, and 16GB/512GB. Expected to be priced around ₹39,999 (dropping to roughly ₹37,000 with card offers), the V60 is not aimed at hardcore performance gamers. Instead, it is a beautifully designed, camera-centric smartphone with an incredibly reliable battery—perfect for users who prioritize photography, aesthetics, and multimedia consumption.
