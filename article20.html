Motorola H70 Pro Plus Review: A Highly Balanced Flagship Experience

June 12, 2026 By Tech Team 6 Min Read

The premium mid-range smartphone segment is arguably the most competitive space in the market right now. The newly launched Motorola H70 Pro Plus enters this arena with an effective price hovering under ₹45,000. In a market where manufacturers frequently cut corners, Motorola delivers a surprisingly balanced device. Here is an in-depth technical review of the H70 Pro Plus.

1. Design, Build, and Durability

The H70 Pro Plus embraces a remarkably slim profile, measuring in at a surprisingly light 188.3 grams. This low weight is a significant engineering achievement considering the massive 6500mAh battery housed within. The rear panel features a premium satin finish and is available in distinct colors like Maroon, Coffee, and Blue.

Motorola has not compromised on durability. The device features:

  • Glass Protection: Corning Gorilla Glass 7i protects the quad-curved display.
  • Structural Integrity: Military Standard 810H certification ensures high drop-resistance.
  • Water/Dust Resistance: IP68 and IP69 ratings ensure the phone is well-protected against water and dust.

2. A Borderless Display Experience

The screen-to-body ratio on this device is phenomenal, sitting around 96.7% or 96.8%. The 6.8-inch Extreme AMOLED display supports a 144Hz refresh rate and HDR10+.

Brightness is top-tier, boasting a 5200 nits peak brightness, with High Brightness Mode (HBM) hitting approximately 1700 to 1800 nits. Additionally, it features "Smart Water Touch" technology, ensuring the screen remains responsive even with water droplets on it—perfect for monsoon season usage.

3. Performance and Hardware Architecture

Motorola has opted for a single, powerful memory configuration: 12GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 256GB of UFS 4.1 storage. The processing power comes from the Dimensity 8500 Extreme chipset, which delivers an AnTuTu benchmark score of roughly 2.1 to 2.2 Million.

Gaming and Thermals: Hardcore gamers will appreciate the ability to run titles like BGMI and COD Mobile at 120 FPS. Thermal management is handled by a massive 4600 mm² vapor cooling chamber, which effectively keeps peak temperatures around 40-41°C during extended gaming sessions.

4. Battery and Charging Suite

The device packs a substantial 6500mAh battery. The retail box includes a 90W USB Type-C to Type-C charger. Furthermore, unlike the standard Pro model, the Pro Plus variant supports 15W wireless charging, rounding out a complete flagship charging experience.

5. A Complete 50MP Camera Setup

Unlike many competitors that downgrade secondary lenses, the H70 Pro Plus features a highly versatile and completely functional camera system:

  • Primary Sensor: 50MP Sony sensor capturing excellent dynamic range and accurate Pantone-validated skin tones.
  • Telephoto: 50MP sensor with 3.5x optical zoom. Note: Early software versions showed slightly soft telephoto images, which are expected to be fixed via an upcoming OTA update.
  • Ultrawide: 50MP sensor with autofocus, enabling high-resolution macro photography.
  • Front Camera: A 50MP selfie shooter.

Impressively, all four sensors (including the macro functionality) support 4K 60FPS video recording.

6. Software and AI Integration

The device runs on Hello UI based on Android 16, with a solid commitment of 3 years of major OS updates and 5 years of security patches. The UI is exceptionally fluid, free of the micro-stutters occasionally seen on older Motorola devices.

Motorola’s approach to AI is pragmatic. Features are deeply integrated into the native workflow—such as playlist generation during ticket bookings—rather than acting as intrusive standalone apps. A dedicated physical AI key on the right side allows users to summon their preferred assistant model (e.g., Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot) instantly.

7. Connectivity and Extras

The H70 Pro Plus is well-equipped with Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.4, and support for 17 5G bands. It features an optical in-display fingerprint sensor, which, due to its lower positioning on the large display, requires a slight ergonomic adjustment. While it lacks expandable storage, an IR blaster, and eSIM support, it provides USB 2.0 connectivity.

Final Verdict and Pricing

Priced initially at ₹47,999, the effective price drops to under ₹45,000 (around ₹44,999) after applying roughly ₹3,000 in bank offers. For users seeking a slightly more affordable option, the regular Edge 70 Pro remains a compelling choice with an effective price of ₹36,999. Ultimately, the Motorola H70 Pro Plus stands out as a highly balanced device with no glaring omissions, making it a robust contender in the premium mid-range segment.
